Decide what happens when someone asks a question that isn't covered by your Knowledge Base.

After an escalation...

When the AI escalates a conversation, it can either keep replying normally or go quiet for that user in that channel until staff handle it.

Max Replies Per Ticket

Limit how many times the AI may reply inside a single ticket. Once the limit is reached the bot stops replying and escalates.

Working Hours

∞ 24/7

Control when the bot responds to messages. By default the bot runs 24/7. Switch to 'Only during working hours' to keep it inside a weekly schedule. Outside those hours the bot stays silent: it does not reply, no credits are spent and no AI is used.

Working hours are disabled. The bot is available 24/7.

Bot Availability

Choose whether the bot is always available or restricted to your weekly schedule. Turning working hours off keeps your schedule saved for later.
